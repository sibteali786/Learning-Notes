## Structure
```
Para 1 (Introduction — 2-3 sentences)
  - Paraphrase the question (don't copy it)
  - State your clear position (thesis) — pick ONE side, don't hedge

Para 2 (Body 1 — main argument FOR your position)
  - Topic sentence stating the reason
  - Explain/develop the reason
  - Concrete example (real-world, hypothetical, or personal)

Para 3 (Body 2 — second argument, OR acknowledge the other side then rebut)
  - Topic sentence stating a second reason
  - Explain/develop
  - Concrete example

Para 4 (Conclusion — 1-2 sentences)
  - Restate your position (paraphrased, not copied from intro)
  - No new arguments here
```
