# Structure

```
Para 1: Paraphrase + state that both views will be discussed (+ optionally hint at your opinion)
Para 2: View A - fully developed with example
Para 3: View B - fully developed with example
Para 4: Conclusion - state YOUR opinion clearly, weighing the two
```

## Practice 1

**Write about the following topic:**

_Some people believe that unpaid community service should be a compulsory part of high school programs. Others believe students should be free to choose how they spend their free time._

Write ≥250 words. Remember: genuinely develop both sides before landing on your own view.

```
Community service is something people in almost all countries chose to perform whether by choice or systematic manner depeding upon the situation. School and Universities usually have specific societies or clubs which promote community work and its mostly optional sometimes required as well.

Every society agrees on one notion that helping those in need is a must attribute to be possesed its' people. We also know that high school is a step stone for students to get introduced to how the real world works. That is why learning to serve others without any monetary interest teaches them an important lesson 'Humanity is above all else'. It makes them realize that life is not only about becoming successfull in terms of money. It also makes them understand why helpful to others without any expection in return is a wonderful virtue.

It is true that all above reasons are valid and justified according to their own perspective but allowing students to chose what they want is also neccessary and important part of well established societies. Freedom of will is something we believe in strongly and it gives students' the right to act according to their own will. Students might want to spend their free time in learning a new skill, spending time with their family, taking care of any of their loved ones in need and playing with their friends etc. We cannot deny these and many other such activities are essential part of a persons' life and are also equally important in shaping him/her to become what they really want.

In conclusion, i firmly believe that community service should be compulsory for high school students unless its not disturbing their lives in any harmful way because it can definitely teach them to be humble, kind and mature to become successfull in life.
```
