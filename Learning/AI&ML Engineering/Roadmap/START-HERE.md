## Your first 30 minutes

1. Read **Start Here** in the playbook (10 minutes) and take the 2-minute track diagnostic.
2. Set up **one** tracker:
   - **Notion:** open the template link, click **Duplicate**, then fill in your profile on the Dashboard page.
   - **Google Sheets:** upload the `.xlsx` to Google Drive → right-click → Open with → Google Sheets → File → Save as Google Sheets.
   - **Excel:** open the `.xlsx` directly.
3. Enter your start date and weekly hours. Find your starting month in the 12-Month Roadmap chapter.
4. Block your week using the Weekly Operating System chapter.
5. Start this month's build. Do not wait until you "finish learning".
