📝 Summarizing Text with LLMs

Turning long text into short, targeted takeaways — with control over length, focus, and summarize-vs-extract mode.
Length Control Focus / Audience Targeting Summarize vs. Extract Batch Processing
Why This Matters

No one has time to read everything. LLMs let you compress long text (articles, reviews, reports) into short digests — a common building block across many real products, e.g. an e-commerce dashboard that summarizes hundreds of customer reviews so a team can scan them quickly and click into full reviews only when needed.

1 · Basic Summary
"Got this panda plush toy for my daughter's birthday, who loves it and takes it everywhere... it's a bit small for what I paid though... arrived a day earlier than expected."
Your task is to generate a short summary of a product review from an e-commerce site. Summarize the review below, in at most 30 words.

→ Output: "Soft and cute panda plush toy loved by daughter, but small for the price. Arrived early."

2 · Focus the Summary by Audience

Same review, same length limit — but adding a "focus on X" clause reshapes which details rise to the top.

🚚 Shipping Dept

"Focus on shipping and delivery" → summary leads with "arrived a day earlier than expected."

💰 Pricing Dept

"Focus on price and perceived value" → summary leads with "price may be too high for the size."

🧸 Product/CX Dept

Could focus on comfort, durability, daughter's reaction — same technique, different angle.

🧪 Scenario: One raw review, three different departments each get a summary tuned to what they actually need to act on — no manual re-reading required.
3 · Summarize vs. Extract
ModePrompt StyleResult
Summarize "Generate a summary focused on shipping" Shipping detail + some other general info too (broader, may include extra context)
Extract "Extract information relevant to shipping" Only: "Product arrived a day earlier than expected." (narrow, on-topic only)

Rule of thumb: use summarize for a general gist, extract when you want strictly on-topic facts with no extra noise.

for review in reviews: prompt = f"""Summarize the review in at most 20 words: ```{review}```""" response = get_completion(prompt) print(response)
🧪 Scenario: Reviews for a panda toy, a lamp, an electric toothbrush, and a blender → each condensed to one line, letting someone scan a whole dashboard of products in seconds instead of reading paragraphs per item.
💡 Interview/practical tip: When designing a summarization feature, always ask "summary for whom, and how short?" — the same source text should be re-prompted per audience/department rather than trying to write one universal summary. And choose extract-mode when precision matters more than context.
