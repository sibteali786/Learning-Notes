## 1 % effect
- Improving by 1 percent each day can induce 37% improvement over a whole year.
- Getting worse each day has also the same effect that we may nearly reach zero after a year if we keep following this trajectory.
- 1% improvements in many small things accumulate to a large betterment overall.
- We should focus on trajectory of our path either its getting better 1% each day or worse each day since at end of each year it gets compounded.
##  Plateau of Latent Potential
- The point in our struggle or habbit when we see actual results.
- Before reaching this point comes the Valley of Despair
###  Valley of Despair
- The duration where our efforts feel useless, everyday feels like we are doing nothing. 
- Not even a small change we can see or results for our hard work we can notice.
- This is `Valley of Despair` , the toughest part of making a good habbit or leaving a bad one.
## Forget About Goals and Focus on System
- If a player only focuses on the scoreboard ( his goal to win a Match ) he would never score anything, but if he/she focuses on the game itself ( system to make a score ) he will end up making alot of score.
- So System is important i.e the way we do something, the habbits we follow, the ideas we generate and the actions we take to achieve something matters not focusing on what we want to achieve.
- We should spend more time designing system not focusing on goals.
### Problem  1: Winners and losers both have same goal
- But who wins in the end ? the one who who followed a system of continuous small improvements or the one who just focused on winning ? off-course the first one.  
### Problem 2: Achieving goal is a momentary change
- Change the system that produces/ caused the result not the result itself.
### Problem 3: Goals Restrict your Happiness
- When we set a goal with restriction that if i do it i am happy, if i don't i'll be a failure, thats catastrophic.
- Because now we fear our goals not interested in it.
- focus on building a system or habit not associating goal with fixed set of outcome success / failure.
- There can be many outcomes possible.

- Achieving a goal should not stop us from continuing our efforts if it does , we are on wrong track.
- Building a system of continual improvement is what makes life better not acheiving a single goal and then resting.

### The Science of How Habit Works
Cue -> Craving -> Response -> Reward
#### Cue
- First indication or signal to where a reward is located around us.
#### Craving
- The desire to make change in our internal state.
- What we crave is not the habit itself but the change in state it delivers.
#### Response
- As it sounds, its the thought or action we take for having a craving.
#### Reward
- Is what we for our response.
- First three steps cause behavior and with all four the behavior is repeated, if reward is not worth it it will not become a habit.
### Four Laws of Behavior Change
Whenever we to change our behavior ask four things
1. How to make it obvious ( cue )
2. How to make it attractive ( craving )
3. How to make it easy ( Response )
4. How to make it satisfying ( Reward )

### The best way to start a new habit
#### Implementation Intention
- *The plan we make beforehand about where and when to act.*
- For instance I will exercise daily at 4 pm to 6 pm at Gym. 
- here when = Daily at 4pm and 6pm while Where is Gym. 
The format for creating Implementation Intention is 
`When Situation X Arises, I will perform response Y.`
- Most of time we just wish for something like myself, I wrote on a sticky note saying `I will get AWS cloud certifications this year` 
- When and how are missing here ? i have no plan just a wish to do so ? 
- as per Implementation Intention plan should be there along with the intention that is only when it will turn into a habit.
Formula for this

> [!FORMULA]
> `I will [BEHAVIOR] at [TIME] in [LOCATION]`

like I will exercise for one hour at 5 pm in my local gym.
#### Habit Stacking
- **Diderot Effect** -> *Obtaining a new possession leads us into a loop of further purchases.*

> [!Intuition]
> Identify an already existing habit and stack your new habit on top of it ( taking advantage of connectedness )

- Its a special form of [[#Implementation Intention]] and where we pair habit with current habit instead of time and location. 
> [!FORMULA]
> After [CURRENT HABBIT], I will [NEW HABBIT]

like After i finish my work day i will check on my partner / fiance to know how she is doing.
