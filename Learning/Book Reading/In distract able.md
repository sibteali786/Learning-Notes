## Summary of 1st Chapter
- Distraction stops us from achieving our goals. Any action that move us away from what we really want.
- Traction is any action that leads us to what we really want.
- Trigger either external or internal prompt us into action with cues within our environment or within in.

## Part 1: Master Internal Triggers
### What really motivates us ? 
#### Summary
- We think our motivation is driven by reward and punishment. 
- But in reality its much simpler
> Even when we think we seek pleasure we are actually driven by the desire of freeing ourselves from pain of wanting.
- The distraction is actually our way to escape the real problem at hand. It might give us feeling of being in control and succeed while in real life we might be a failure.
- Understanding root cause of distraction is essential as proximate cause may seem the issue at hand but it might be entirely different from root cause.
