I start from the actual problem, not the ticket. On Asia Builders ERP, the ask was "give the accountant a dashboard and owner a way to tell how much profit they got and loss they incurred", but the real problem was 40 hours a month lost to Excel and no way to answer "who owes us money right now." I design the data model around that question first, build the smallest end-to-end slice that answers it, then get it in front of the real user before adding the rest. I write tests for the failure paths, not just the happy path, a lot of my architecture decisions (like isolating dashboard queries so one failure doesn't blank the screen) came from watching something break in production and designing so it cannot happen again.
