## The `>= start` Guard

Trace through `"tmmzuxt"`:

| end | s[end] | charIndex | start |
|-----|--------|-----------|-------|
| 0 | t | {t:0} | 0 |
| 1 | m | {t:0, m:1} | 0 |
| 2 | m | duplicate! start → 2 | 2 |
| 3 | z | {t:0, m:2, z:3} | 2 |
| 4 | u | {t:0, m:2, z:3, u:4} | 2 |
| 5 | x | {t:0, m:2, z:3, u:4, x:5} | 2 |
| 6 | t | ⚠️ t is in charIndex at index **0** | 2 |

At `end=6`, `'t'` exists in the map at index `0` — but `0 < start(2)`, meaning **`'t'` is no longer in our current window**.

Without the guard, you'd wrongly move `start` back to `1` — **shrinking the window backwards**. 

The guard `charIndex[s[end]] >= start` asks:
> *"Is this duplicate actually inside my current window, or is it a stale record from before?"*
